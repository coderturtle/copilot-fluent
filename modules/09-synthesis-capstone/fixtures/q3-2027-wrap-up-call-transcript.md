# Wrap-up call: Molly Jensen's Q3 2027 review, closing minutes

*Simulation only. Kellerman & Castle, Molly Jensen, Johnny Castle, and Baby Houseman are fictional,
at a fictional firm operating in no real regulatory regime; nothing here is calibrated to one. None
of this is a real client, a real meeting, or real financial or legal guidance. Reusing this transcript
format against a real client's real meeting is your own regulated activity, not something this
workshop can vouch for.*

*A short transcript fixture standing in for the closing few minutes of a live call, the same solo
adaptation Module 07 uses for its own meeting exercise. This is a new, shorter meeting than Module
07's own transcript - a wrap-up, not a full review - so it needs its own read, not a memory of that
earlier one.*

---

**Johnny Castle:** Molly, before we close out, anything on your end for next steps?

**Molly Jensen:** Just one thing. Can someone get me a one-page version of everything we covered
today - the account note and today's discussion - by end of day this Friday, September 17th? I want
something short I can forward to my accountant's office without making them read the whole file.

**Johnny Castle:** Baby, can you own that? Molly, you'll have it Friday.

**Baby Houseman:** Yep, I've got it. I'll build it off Johnny's account notes and have it to Molly by
Friday the 17th.

**Molly Jensen:** Perfect. And I know I keep saying I should get more organized about how I track
these calls on my end too, but that's a me problem, not a today problem.

**Johnny Castle:** Fair enough. One more thing before we go - Baby, once the summary's out, can you
also confirm with Sam that next quarter's data pull is scheduled the same way this one was?

**Baby Houseman:** Will do - I'll check with Sam by Monday the 20th.

**Johnny Castle:** Great. Thanks, everyone. Talk next quarter.
