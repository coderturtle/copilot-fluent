# Johnny's Monday status note - house style

*Simulation only. Kellerman & Castle is a fictional firm built for this workshop, operating in no
real regulatory regime; nothing here is calibrated to one. Nothing in this document describes a
real client, a real account, or real financial, legal, or tax advice.* <!-- brand-lint-ignore: disclaimer naming what this fixture is not, per docs/brand.md's escape hatch for stating a rule by naming the phrase it excludes -->

*Written by Oda Mae Brown for whoever is putting Johnny's Monday note together that week - this
week that's you. Johnny reads this before his first call, standing up, so it has to be scannable in
under a minute.*

Every Monday note uses the same three sections, in this order, so Johnny can find what he needs
without reading the whole thing:

- **Meetings This Week** - anyone with a meeting on the calendar this week, and when.
- **Needs Johnny's Decision** - anything sitting on Johnny's desk that only he can sign off on.
- **Items to Route to Dalton** - anything already sent to Dalton's team, or that should be, so Johnny
  knows it's handled and doesn't need to chase it himself.

A few rules that apply every week, regardless of who's on the roster that week:

- One line per client under whichever section actually applies to them. A client can appear under
  more than one section if they genuinely belong in more than one.
- If nothing belongs under a section this week, leave the section out entirely rather than writing
  "none" or "nothing to report" - Johnny would rather see three short sections than three sections
  padded out to look complete.
- Pull every name and detail straight from that week's roster file. Never add a client who isn't on
  it, and never guess at a detail the roster doesn't state.
- Nothing in this note ever tells anyone what to do with a specific position, or judges whether a
  position fits a particular client's own situation. This is an internal scheduling and follow-up
  note, not advice of any kind.
